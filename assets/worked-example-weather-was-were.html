Sentence Factory

Send each subject down a lane. Watch the factory choose the word that makes the sentence work.

Choose a subject token

Choose a token. Where will it go?

🔵 WAS lane

The single signals travel here:

I · he · she · it → was

It was rainy.   Mia was cold.

🟠 WERE lane

The “you” and group signals travel here:

you · we · they → were

We were at school.   They were happy.

Switch OFF a fact

It wasn’t cloudy.
We weren’t cold.

was not → wasn’t · were not → weren’t

Turn a fact into a question

Was it windy?   Yes, it was.
Were you at school?   No, I wasn’t.

Move Was / Were to the front.

Factory discovery: Weather descriptions usually begin with it: “It was rainy yesterday.” 天气描述中常用 it 作主语。
